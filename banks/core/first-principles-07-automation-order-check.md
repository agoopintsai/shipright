## Question

Applies when the plan automates or speeds up a process. Did the earlier steps happen first: questioned (who truly needs it to run this fast or automatically), deleted (which steps could simply be dropped), and simplified (is the current shape the simplest one)? What is the evidence?

## Why it matters

Automating a process that should not exist, or is still bloated, only makes its errors run faster and harder to stop. The order matters: question, delete, simplify, and only then accelerate and automate.
