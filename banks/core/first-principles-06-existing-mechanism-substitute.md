## Question

Could an existing feature, report, setting, or manual process (or a small tweak to one) cover 80% of the need? Is the remaining 20% worth a new mechanism?

## Why it matters

Each new mechanism brings new consumers, new safety assumptions, and a new incident surface. Adding something new is always more tempting than understanding the old thing, because understanding it means reading legacy code.
