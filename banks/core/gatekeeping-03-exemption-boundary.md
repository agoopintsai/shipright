## Question

For every exemption lane in the plan (trivial change, proof of concept, fast track, allowlist): who decides that a change belongs to the exempt class? If the party being exempted classifies itself, where is the backstop for misclassification and how long until it is noticed? Is the exemption an enumerated list named by the authority, or criteria the executor applies?

## Why it matters

Exemption lanes are the default hole in any gate design. "This is probably trivial" is where self-classification hides, and a substantive change can skip every control in its name. Every criterion-based rule is a doorway that can be eroded one case at a time.
