## Question

If we do nothing at all, who hurts in three months, and how much? Give numbers against the restated problem: how many users, how many transactions, how much money, how many hours of manual work. If no magnitude can be given, record the question as unanswered and treat it as a blocker.

## Why it matters

Priority that cannot be quantified comes from emotion, not data. Every change carries an incident probability and a maintenance cost; a change whose benefit has no stated magnitude has a negative expected value by default.
