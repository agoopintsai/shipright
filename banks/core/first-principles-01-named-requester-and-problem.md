## Question

Who, by name, asked for this, and what problem (not what solution) did they describe in their own words? If the AI derived the requirement itself, say so explicitly.

## Why it matters

A requirement that names a solution locks out cheaper alternatives. A requirement attributed to a department or to "everyone" cannot be questioned, because nobody owns it. Requirements from clever people or from an AI-written plan are the most dangerous: nobody dares to challenge them.
