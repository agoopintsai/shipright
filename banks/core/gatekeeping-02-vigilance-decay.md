## Question

For every ongoing human check of AI output (review, spot check), how is vigilance decay handled structurally? Is the sampling rhythm independent of the AI's recent track record ("it has been right lately" is a reason to sample more, not less)? Does someone other than the reviewed party choose the sample? Does the design rely on the reviewer's own sense of how careful they are?

## Why it matters

The more reliable automation becomes, the less people catch its misses (detection falling from roughly 75% to 30% in classic monitoring studies). Developers using AI assistants have been shown to write less secure code while believing it is more secure, so self-assessment is unreliable. Willpower decays; only structural sampling survives.

## Origin

Perry et al. 2023; Parasuraman et al. 1993.
