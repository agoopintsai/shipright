## Question

For each safety premise in the plan, who or what can break it after launch: manual data fixes, repair scripts, another scheduled job, a rollback, or the next unrelated deploy?

## Why it matters

Treating a premise as "true on launch day" turns a continuing guarantee into a one-time check. Out-of-band operations are the usual way premises silently stop being true.
