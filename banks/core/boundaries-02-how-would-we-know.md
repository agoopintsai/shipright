## Question

When the premise stops holding, how do we find out, and how long does that take: an alert, a reconciliation job, a database constraint, or only when downstream damage surfaces?

## Why it matters

A premise with no detection means that once it fails, it quietly feeds bad data to everything that trusts it. Damage grows with the time between failure and discovery.
